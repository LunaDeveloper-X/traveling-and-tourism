About Japan

Japan is an island country in East Asia known for its ancient traditions, modern cities, beautiful mountains, cherry blossoms, temples and unique cuisine.

  • Capital: Tokyo
  • Largest City: Tokyo
  • Region: East Asia
  • Famous For: Technology, anime, temples and cuisine
  • Popular Destination: Tokyo and Kyoto

Famous Places in Japan

Explore some of Japan's most famous cities and beautiful destinations.

Tokyo

Tokyo is Japan's capital and one of the world's largest and most exciting metropolitan cities.

About Tokyo

Tokyo is famous for Shibuya Crossing, Tokyo Tower, Senso-ji Temple, shopping districts, technology and Japanese pop culture.

Kyoto

Kyoto is famous for traditional Japanese temples, gardens, shrines and historic neighborhoods.

About Kyoto

Kyoto was the imperial capital of Japan for many centuries. It is known for traditional wooden houses, temples, gardens and cultural heritage.

Mount Fuji

Mount Fuji is Japan's highest mountain and one of the country's most recognizable natural landmarks.

About Mount Fuji

Mount Fuji is a famous symbol of Japan. Its symmetrical volcanic shape has inspired Japanese art and culture for centuries.

Osaka

Osaka is a lively Japanese city known for food, entertainment, shopping and Osaka Castle.

About Osaka

Osaka is known for its friendly atmosphere, street food, shopping districts and landmarks such as Osaka Castle.

Historical Places in Japan

Discover Japan's ancient temples, castles and traditional cultural landmarks.

Fushimi Inari Shrine

A famous Kyoto shrine recognized for its thousands of bright torii gates.

Historical Information

Fushimi Inari is one of Kyoto's best-known Shinto shrines and is famous for its long paths lined with torii gates.

Kinkaku-ji

Kinkaku-ji, also known as the Golden Pavilion, is one of Kyoto's most famous landmarks.

Historical Information

Kinkaku-ji is a historic Zen Buddhist temple in Kyoto and is particularly famous for its gold-covered upper floors.

Hiroshima Peace Memorial

A significant historical site that represents the history and remembrance of Hiroshima.

Historical Information

The Hiroshima Peace Memorial area serves as a place of remembrance and promotes the message of peace.

Famous Restaurants in Japan

Experience Japanese cuisine through traditional and modern dining experiences.

Traditional Sushi Restaurant

Japan is famous for sushi restaurants that focus on fresh ingredients and careful preparation.

Restaurant Information

Traditional sushi restaurants focus on fresh seafood, carefully prepared rice and precise presentation.

Ramen Restaurant

Ramen restaurants are popular throughout Japan, offering different regional styles of noodles and broth.

Restaurant Information

Ramen shops are a major part of Japanese food culture, with many regions offering their own styles and flavors.

Izakaya

Izakayas are Japanese casual dining establishments offering a variety of small dishes and drinks.

Restaurant Information

Izakayas provide a relaxed atmosphere and commonly serve a variety of Japanese dishes designed for sharing.

Famous Japanese Food

Japanese cuisine is famous around the world for its variety, presentation and fresh ingredients.

Sushi

Sushi is one of Japan's most famous foods and is prepared in many different styles.

About Sushi

Sushi generally combines seasoned rice with seafood, vegetables or other ingredients and is prepared in many regional styles.

Ramen

Ramen is a popular Japanese noodle dish served with different broths and toppings.

About Ramen

Ramen consists of noodles served in broth with toppings such as meat, vegetables, eggs and other ingredients.

Tempura

Tempura is made by lightly battering and frying seafood or vegetables.

About Tempura

Tempura is a Japanese cooking style in which seafood or vegetables are coated in a light batter and fried until crisp.

Takoyaki

Takoyaki is a popular Japanese street food especially associated with Osaka.

About Takoyaki

Takoyaki consists of round batter-based pieces traditionally containing octopus and served with sauces and toppings.
