About South Korea

South Korea combines ancient traditions with modern technology and vibrant city life. From the busy streets of Seoul to peaceful temples and beautiful coastal areas, the country offers a wide variety of experiences.

  • Capital: Seoul
  • Largest City: Seoul
  • Region: East Asia
  • Famous For: K-pop, technology, food and culture
  • Popular Destination: Seoul and Jeju Island

Famous Places in South Korea

Discover some of the most popular destinations in South Korea.

Seoul

Seoul is the capital of South Korea and a lively city where historic palaces stand alongside modern skyscrapers and shopping districts.

About Seoul

Seoul is the political, cultural and economic center of South Korea. Famous attractions include Gyeongbokgung Palace, N Seoul Tower, Myeongdong, Bukchon Hanok Village and the Han River.

Busan

Busan is a coastal city famous for beaches, seafood, colorful neighborhoods and beautiful ocean views.

About Busan

Busan is South Korea's major port city and is known for Haeundae Beach, Gamcheon Culture Village, seafood markets and beautiful coastal scenery.

Jeju Island

Jeju Island is known for volcanic landscapes, waterfalls, beaches and beautiful natural scenery.

About Jeju Island

Jeju is a volcanic island famous for Hallasan Mountain, waterfalls, lava formations and peaceful coastal scenery.

Gyeongju

Gyeongju is a historic city filled with ancient temples, royal tombs and traditional Korean heritage.

About Gyeongju

Gyeongju was the capital of the ancient Silla Kingdom and contains many important cultural and historical sites.

Historical Places in South Korea

Explore South Korea's historic palaces, temples and traditional villages.

Gyeongbokgung Palace

A magnificent royal palace and one of Seoul's most important historical attractions.

Historical Information

Gyeongbokgung is one of Seoul's most important royal palaces and an important symbol of Korean history and architecture.

Bukchon Hanok Village

A traditional neighborhood in Seoul filled with beautiful Korean houses known as hanok.

Historical Information

Bukchon Hanok Village preserves traditional Korean architecture and provides a glimpse into historic Seoul.

Bulguksa Temple

A famous Buddhist temple connected with the history and culture of the Silla period.

Historical Information

Bulguksa is an important Buddhist temple and UNESCO World Heritage site associated with the Silla Kingdom.

Famous Restaurants in South Korea

Experience Korean cuisine at some well-known dining destinations.

Gwangjang Market

A famous Seoul market where visitors can enjoy traditional Korean street food.

Restaurant Information

Gwangjang Market is one of Seoul's famous food destinations, offering traditional Korean snacks, noodles and other local dishes.

Mingles

A Seoul restaurant known for modern interpretations of Korean cuisine.

Restaurant Information

Mingles is associated with contemporary Korean cuisine and creative presentations of traditional Korean ingredients.

Korean Barbecue Restaurant

Korean barbecue restaurants offer a popular dining experience where meat is grilled at the table.

Restaurant Information

Korean barbecue is a popular dining style in which diners grill meat themselves at the table and enjoy it with side dishes.

Famous Korean Food

Korean cuisine is famous for bold flavors, fermented foods, rice dishes and delicious street food.

Kimchi

Kimchi is a traditional Korean fermented vegetable dish and one of the country's most famous foods.

About Kimchi

Kimchi is traditionally made by fermenting vegetables with seasonings. It is served as a side dish with many Korean meals.

Bibimbap

Bibimbap is a Korean rice dish usually served with vegetables, meat, egg and flavorful sauce.

About Bibimbap

Bibimbap combines rice with vegetables, protein and sauce. The ingredients are mixed together before eating.

Tteokbokki

Tteokbokki is a popular Korean street food made from chewy rice cakes cooked in a spicy sauce.

About Tteokbokki

Tteokbokki is made with rice cakes and is commonly cooked in a spicy and slightly sweet Korean sauce.

Korean Barbecue

Korean barbecue is a famous dining experience featuring grilled meat and a variety of side dishes.

About Korean Barbecue

Korean barbecue commonly features grilled meat served with vegetables, sauces and numerous traditional side dishes.
