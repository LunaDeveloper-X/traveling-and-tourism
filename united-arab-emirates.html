About United Arab Emirates

The United Arab Emirates is a country in the Arabian Peninsula known for its modern cities, desert landscapes, traditional culture and famous landmarks.

The United Arab Emirates is made up of seven emirates. Dubai and Abu Dhabi are among its best-known cities. Visitors can experience skyscrapers, traditional markets, desert adventures, beaches, museums and historic buildings.
