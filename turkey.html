About Turkey

Turkey is one of the world's most fascinating travel destinations. It is famous for its rich history, beautiful architecture, diverse landscapes and delicious cuisine. Visitors can explore modern cities, ancient ruins, beaches, mountains and traditional markets.

  • Capital: Ankara
  • Largest City: Istanbul
  • Region: Europe and Asia
  • Famous For: History, culture, food and landscapes
  • Popular Destination: Istanbul and Cappadocia

Famous Places in Turkey

Explore some of Turkey's most beautiful and popular destinations.

Istanbul

Istanbul is Turkey's largest city and a unique destination connecting Europe and Asia. The city is famous for historic buildings, the Bosphorus, markets and beautiful mosques.

About Istanbul

Istanbul has played an important role in history for centuries. Important attractions include Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, Topkapi Palace, Grand Bazaar and the Bosphorus.

Cappadocia

Cappadocia is famous for its unusual rock formations, cave homes, underground cities and unforgettable hot-air balloon rides.

About Cappadocia

Cappadocia's landscape was created by volcanic activity and erosion. It is especially famous for its fairy chimneys and hot-air balloon tours.

Pamukkale

Pamukkale is famous for its brilliant white mineral terraces and natural thermal pools.

About Pamukkale

Pamukkale means "cotton castle". Its white terraces are formed by mineral-rich thermal waters flowing down the hillside.

Antalya

Antalya is a beautiful Mediterranean destination known for beaches, resorts, waterfalls and historic attractions.

About Antalya

Antalya is located on Turkey's Mediterranean coast. It is popular for its beaches, old town, waterfalls and nearby ancient ruins.

Historical Places in Turkey

Turkey has a fascinating history and is home to many ancient landmarks and archaeological sites.

Hagia Sophia

One of Istanbul's most famous historical landmarks, known for its enormous dome and remarkable history.

Historical Information

Hagia Sophia is one of Istanbul's most important architectural landmarks and has served different religious and cultural roles throughout history.

Blue Mosque

The Blue Mosque is famous for its beautiful domes, minarets and detailed interior decoration.

Historical Information

The Blue Mosque is one of Istanbul's most recognizable landmarks and is known for its impressive Ottoman architecture.

Ephesus

Ephesus is an ancient city famous for its impressive ruins and classical architecture.

Historical Information

Ephesus was an important ancient city and is today one of Turkey's most significant archaeological destinations.

Famous Restaurants in Turkey

Discover restaurants and dining experiences associated with Turkish cuisine.

Mikla

A well-known Istanbul restaurant associated with modern Turkish cuisine and city views.

Restaurant Information

Mikla is known for combining Turkish ingredients and culinary traditions with modern cooking techniques.

Karaköy Lokantası

A popular Istanbul dining destination known for traditional Turkish dishes and a stylish atmosphere.

Restaurant Information

Karaköy Lokantası is associated with traditional Turkish flavors and a modern Istanbul dining experience.

Çiya Sofrası

A famous Istanbul restaurant associated with regional Turkish cooking and traditional recipes.

Restaurant Information

Çiya Sofrası is especially associated with regional Turkish dishes and lesser-known traditional recipes.

Famous Turkish Food

Turkish cuisine is famous for its variety, rich flavors and traditional cooking methods.

Kebab

Kebab is one of the most internationally recognized Turkish foods, prepared in many regional styles.

About Kebab

Turkish kebab comes in many varieties, including grilled meat dishes prepared with different spices, vegetables and cooking techniques.

Baklava

Baklava is a famous sweet pastry made with thin layers, nuts and sweet syrup.

About Baklava

Baklava is prepared using thin pastry layers, chopped nuts and sweet syrup. It is one of the best-known desserts of Turkish cuisine.

Turkish Delight

Turkish delight is a traditional sweet available in many flavors and varieties.

About Turkish Delight

Turkish delight is a traditional confection made in many flavors, sometimes containing nuts or dried fruits.

Turkish Pide

Pide is a traditional Turkish flatbread often topped with meat, cheese or vegetables.

About Turkish Pide

Pide is a popular Turkish bread dish that can be prepared with different toppings such as cheese, meat, eggs and vegetables.
