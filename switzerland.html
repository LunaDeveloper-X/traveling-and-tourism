Switzerland

Discover Switzerland's breathtaking mountains, peaceful lakes, beautiful cities, historic places and delicious Swiss food.

About Switzerland

Switzerland is a beautiful country in Central Europe, famous for the Alps, scenic lakes, charming villages, historic cities and spectacular mountain landscapes. It is also well known for chocolate, cheese, watches and excellent transportation.

Capital

Bern

Languages

German, French, Italian and Romansh

Continent

Europe

Currency

Swiss Franc

Famous Places in Switzerland

Explore Switzerland's beautiful cities, mountains, lakes and unforgettable landscapes.

Zurich

Switzerland's largest city, known for its beautiful old town, lake and cultural attractions.

Zurich combines a historic old town with a modern city atmosphere. Visitors can enjoy Lake Zurich, museums, shopping streets and beautiful views.

Interlaken

A popular destination surrounded by mountains, lakes and beautiful Swiss landscapes.

Interlaken is situated between Lake Thun and Lake Brienz. It is a popular base for exploring the surrounding Alps and enjoying outdoor activities.

Lucerne

A picturesque city famous for its lake, mountains and historic wooden bridge.

Lucerne is one of Switzerland's most scenic cities. The old town, Lake Lucerne and surrounding mountains make it a popular destination for visitors.

Zermatt

A famous mountain resort known for the Matterhorn and spectacular Alpine scenery.

Zermatt is a famous Alpine resort and an excellent destination for mountain scenery, skiing, hiking and views of the Matterhorn.

Historical Places

Discover Switzerland's historic buildings, old towns and cultural landmarks.

Chillon Castle

A historic castle located beside Lake Geneva, surrounded by beautiful Alpine scenery.

Chillon Castle is one of Switzerland's best-known historic monuments. Its location beside Lake Geneva makes it especially scenic.

Old Town of Bern

A historic old town famous for medieval streets, fountains, arcades and traditional buildings.

Bern's historic old town is known for its medieval urban character, arcades and historic architecture. It is one of Switzerland's most important cultural areas.

Zurich Old Town

A charming historic area filled with narrow streets, old buildings and cultural landmarks.

Zurich's old town offers historic architecture, narrow streets, churches, shops and restaurants. It is a popular area for walking and sightseeing.

Famous Restaurants

Discover Swiss dining and traditional cuisine.

Traditional Swiss Restaurant

A traditional dining experience featuring classic Swiss dishes and local specialties.

Traditional Swiss restaurants often serve fondue, raclette, rösti and other regional specialties in a comfortable atmosphere.

Zurich Dining

Zurich offers traditional Swiss dishes along with modern international cuisine.

Zurich has a diverse restaurant scene where visitors can experience traditional Swiss cuisine as well as modern European and international dishes.

Swiss Mountain Restaurant

Enjoy Swiss specialties while surrounded by beautiful mountain scenery.

Mountain restaurants are popular in the Swiss Alps, especially for visitors who want traditional food with spectacular views.

Famous Swiss Food

Taste some of Switzerland's most famous traditional foods.

Cheese Fondue

Melted cheese served in a shared pot, traditionally enjoyed with pieces of bread.

Cheese fondue is one of Switzerland's most famous dishes. It is traditionally prepared with melted cheese and served with bread for dipping.

Raclette

A traditional Swiss dish featuring melted cheese, commonly served with potatoes and vegetables.

Raclette is a traditional Swiss cheese dish. Melted cheese is commonly served with potatoes, vegetables and other accompaniments.

Rösti

A traditional Swiss potato dish that is crispy on the outside and soft inside.

Rösti is a well-known Swiss potato dish. It is made from grated potatoes that are cooked until golden and crispy.

Swiss Chocolate

Switzerland is famous around the world for its high-quality chocolate.

Swiss chocolate has a strong international reputation. Visitors can find chocolate shops and producers in many Swiss cities and tourist destinations.
