About Germany

Germany is a country in Central Europe known for its rich history, beautiful architecture, historic towns, castles, museums and diverse landscapes. From modern cities such as Berlin and Munich to fairy-tale castles and charming old towns, Germany offers many experiences for travelers.

Famous Places in Germany

Explore some of Germany's most popular cities and tourist attractions.

Berlin

Germany's capital city, famous for its history, museums, architecture and vibrant culture.

Berlin is one of Germany's most important cultural and historical cities. Visitors can explore the Brandenburg Gate, Museum Island, the Reichstag and many other attractions.

Neuschwanstein Castle

A famous fairy-tale castle in Bavaria surrounded by beautiful mountains and forests.

Neuschwanstein Castle is one of Germany's best-known castles. Its dramatic mountain setting and romantic architecture make it one of the country's most popular attractions.

Rothenburg ob der Tauber

A charming historic town known for its medieval streets, colorful buildings and traditional atmosphere.

Rothenburg ob der Tauber is famous for its preserved medieval appearance. Its old streets, traditional houses and historic walls make it a popular destination.

Hamburg

A major German city known for its harbor, waterways, architecture and lively cultural scene.

Hamburg is one of Germany's major cities and an important port. The city is known for its harbor, museums, historic buildings and waterfront areas.

Historical Places

Discover Germany's historic landmarks, castles and cultural sites.

Brandenburg Gate

One of Berlin's most recognizable landmarks and an important symbol of German history.

The Brandenburg Gate is one of Berlin's best-known landmarks. It has played an important role in the history and identity of the city.

Eltz Castle

A beautiful medieval castle located in a forested valley in western Germany.

Eltz Castle is known for its medieval architecture and picturesque location. Germany Travel lists it among the country's notable castles and palaces.

Museum Island

A famous cultural area in Berlin containing several important museums.

Museum Island is located in the heart of Berlin and is an important cultural destination with several museums and historic buildings.

Famous Restaurants

Experience German cuisine in traditional restaurants, modern dining spaces and famous food destinations.

Traditional German Restaurant

Enjoy classic German dishes in a traditional restaurant atmosphere.

Traditional German restaurants often serve hearty dishes such as sausages, schnitzel, potatoes and regional specialties.

Berlin Dining

Berlin offers a diverse food scene combining German traditions with international cuisine.

Berlin has a large and diverse culinary scene. Visitors can find traditional German dishes as well as many international and modern restaurants.

Bavarian Restaurant

Taste traditional Bavarian specialties in a warm and welcoming setting.

Bavarian cuisine is known for hearty meals and regional specialties. Munich and Bavaria are especially famous for their traditional food culture.

Famous German Food

Try some of Germany's most famous traditional foods.

Bratwurst

A popular German sausage that comes in many regional varieties.

Bratwurst is one of the best-known German foods. Different regions have their own recipes and styles of preparing this traditional sausage.

Schnitzel

A breaded and fried meat dish commonly served with potatoes or salad.

Schnitzel is widely enjoyed in German-speaking regions. It is commonly served with potatoes, salad or other traditional side dishes.

Pretzel

A traditional baked snack with a distinctive twisted shape and golden-brown crust.

Pretzels are especially popular in southern Germany and are commonly enjoyed as a snack or with traditional meals.

Sauerkraut

Fermented cabbage traditionally served as a side dish with many German meals.

Sauerkraut is a traditional German food made from fermented cabbage. It is often served alongside meat, sausages and potatoes.
