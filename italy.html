Italy

Discover the beauty, history, food and culture of Italy

About Italy

Italy is a beautiful country in Southern Europe famous for its ancient history, art, architecture, fashion, beautiful cities and delicious cuisine. Italy is home to famous destinations such as Rome, Venice, Florence and Milan. The country is also known for landmarks such as the Colosseum, the Leaning Tower of Pisa and the canals of Venice.

Famous Places in Italy

Explore some of the most beautiful and famous destinations in Italy.

Rome

Rome is the capital city of Italy and one of the world's most famous historical cities. It is known for ancient buildings, beautiful fountains and museums.

Rome is often called the Eternal City. Visitors can explore ancient monuments, museums, churches, beautiful squares and traditional Italian streets.

Venice

Venice is famous for its canals, bridges, historic buildings and romantic gondola rides.

Venice is built around a network of canals. It is famous for gondolas, St. Mark's Square, historic bridges and beautiful architecture.

Florence

Florence is famous for Renaissance art, museums, beautiful architecture and historic streets.

Florence played an important role in the Renaissance. It is home to famous museums, galleries, churches and historic buildings.

Milan

Milan is a major Italian city famous for fashion, shopping, art, architecture and business.

Milan is one of Italy's most important cities. It is particularly famous for fashion, the Duomo, shopping districts and cultural attractions.

Historical Places in Italy

Discover Italy's incredible historical landmarks.

Colosseum

The Colosseum is one of the world's most famous ancient Roman landmarks.

The Colosseum was a large amphitheater built in ancient Rome. It remains one of the most recognizable symbols of Roman history.

Pompeii

Pompeii is an ancient Roman city preserved by the eruption of Mount Vesuvius.

Pompeii provides an important look into everyday Roman life. Archaeological remains include homes, streets, public buildings and artwork.

Leaning Tower of Pisa

The Leaning Tower of Pisa is famous around the world because of its distinctive tilt.

The Leaning Tower is the freestanding bell tower of Pisa's cathedral complex. Its famous tilt developed because of unstable foundation conditions.

Famous Restaurants in Italy

Experience Italy through its famous dining traditions.

Roman Restaurant

Traditional Roman restaurants are famous for pasta, pizza, local meat dishes and classic desserts.

Roman dining focuses on simple ingredients, traditional recipes and hearty pasta dishes. Visitors can enjoy food in both modern and traditional restaurants.

Traditional Pizzeria

Italian pizzerias are famous for freshly baked pizza prepared with traditional ingredients.

Traditional Italian pizzerias often focus on fresh dough, tomato, cheese and carefully selected toppings.

Italian Pasta Restaurant

Pasta restaurants offer many traditional Italian pasta dishes prepared with different sauces and ingredients.

Italy has many regional pasta traditions. Different regions use different pasta shapes, sauces, cheeses and ingredients.

Famous Food of Italy

Taste some of the world's most popular Italian foods.

Pizza

Pizza is one of Italy's most famous foods and is enjoyed around the world.

Italian pizza is traditionally made with dough, tomato, cheese and other fresh ingredients. Neapolitan pizza is particularly famous.

Pasta

Pasta is an essential part of Italian cuisine and comes in many shapes and varieties.

Popular Italian pasta dishes include spaghetti, lasagna, ravioli, carbonara and many regional varieties.

Gelato

Gelato is a popular Italian frozen dessert available in many delicious flavors.

Italian gelato is known for its dense, smooth texture and wide range of flavors. Gelaterias can be found throughout Italy.

Tiramisu

Tiramisu is a famous Italian dessert made with coffee, mascarpone and layers of soft biscuits.

Tiramisu is a popular Italian dessert featuring coffee-soaked ladyfingers, mascarpone cream and cocoa.
